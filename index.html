Biography

I am a 4th-year undergraduate in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at Xinjiang University (XJU) (新疆大学). In 2026, I will join the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU) (西安交通大学), for my Master's degree in Applied Mathematics. My research interests span Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, Intelligent Game Theory, and Swarm Intelligence.

I will join the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA) (中科院自动化所) as a research intern in December 2025, working on multi-agent reinforcement learning under the supervision of Prof. Zhiqiang Pu and Prof. Tenghai Qiu. I am passionate about academic research and committed to contributing to the intersection of control systems and artificial intelligence.

I'm open to collaboration—feel free to reach me by e-mail.

Selected Projects

  • Passivity Analysis of Spatiotemporal Networks Based on Sampling Control National Project
    Project Leader | Supervised by Prof. Cheng Hu
    National Innovation Training Program for College Students
    Conducted theoretical analysis and numerical simulations for spatiotemporal neural networks with sampling control. Developed passivity criteria and synchronization conditions, implemented control algorithms in MATLAB. Contributed to two published papers including one SCI Q2 journal article.
    [GitHub]

  • Olympic Medal Prediction Model MCM 2025
    Team Project | Mathematical Contest in Modeling
    LSTM + Bayesian Monte Carlo Dropout
    Built a predictive model combining LSTM networks with Bayesian Monte Carlo Dropout for Olympic medal forecasting. Used XGBoost for median prediction and developed differential models to analyze countries' breakthrough possibilities and coaching impact.
    [GitHub]

  • Super Mario Reinforcement Learning Agent Deep RL
    Individual Project
    DDQN-based Game AI Agent
    Implemented a DDQN-based agent for automatic gameplay in Super Mario Bros environment. Used CNN for processing game frames, applied reward shaping techniques to improve training efficiency, and implemented soft target network updates for stability.
    [GitHub]

  • MALFAC Multi-Agent Interception System Multi-Agent RL
    Research Project
    Direction-Assisted Actor & Level-Fusion Pyramid Critic
    Developed a multi-agent reinforcement learning system for target interception tasks featuring Direction-Assisted Actor networks and Level-Fusion Pyramid Critic architectures. Achieved 98.0% success rate in final 50 episodes through iterative training and algorithm refinement.
    [GitHub]

  • Reinforcement Learning Hub Education

  • StarCraft II Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Game AI
    Individual Project
