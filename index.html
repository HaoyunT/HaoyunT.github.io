Biography

I am a Master's student in Applied Mathematics at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU) (西安交通大学). I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Xinjiang University (XJU) (新疆大学), where my GPA ranked in the top 3%. My research interests span World Models, Reinforcement Learning, Diffusion Models, and Spatial Intelligence.

I completed a research internship at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA) (中科院自动化所) from Nov 2025 to Feb 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Zhiqiang Pu and Prof. Tenghai Qiu, where I worked on multi-agent reinforcement learning, world-model navigation, and embodied control. I am currently an autonomous driving algorithm intern at Meituan（美团）'s Autonomous Vehicle Business Unit, focusing on multimodal trajectory generation and motion planning with Flow Matching, Energy Field modeling, and reinforcement learning for autonomous delivery vehicles. I am passionate about research that connects mathematically grounded modeling with real-world intelligent systems.

I'm open to collaboration—feel free to reach me by e-mail.
