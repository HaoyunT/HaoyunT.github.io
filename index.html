Hi, welcome to my homepage.

Biography

I am an M.S. student in Applied Mathematics at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU). My research interests include World Action Models, Reinforcement Learning, and Embodied Intelligence.

I have worked on embodied reinforcement learning and world-model navigation at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA); at Meituan, I worked on large-scale pretraining for multimodal trajectory generation with Flow Matching, followed by post-training with Energy-Field guidance and reinforcement learning; and at LinkerBot, I worked on DexLeWM post-training to adapt pretrained VLA/WAM capabilities to coordinated dexterous manipulation.

I am interested in building unified world representations for robots. I welcome research discussions and academic collaboration; feel free to reach me by e-mail.

I am currently seeking research internship opportunities. If you think my background could be a good fit for your team or project, please feel free to contact me by e-mail.

Research & Industry Experience

  • LinkerBot 2026.06 – 2026.09
    Embodied AI Algorithm Intern Evo Lab · Beijing
    Developing DexLeWM for multi-task fine manipulation with a 7-DoF arm and 16-DoF dexterous hand by adapting pretrained Being-H0.5-style arm-gripper VLA/WAM capabilities. Built action adapters, dimension masks, and decoupled Arm/Hand Experts with arm-to-hand attention and event gating. Aligned action representations with effect latents from future visual observations through Action–Future Effect Alignment and posterior–prior distillation, and incorporated multi-horizon chunks and episodic memory for robust real-robot control.
  • Meituan 2026.03 – 2026.06
    Autonomous Driving Algorithm Intern Autonomous Delivery Business Unit · Beijing
    Worked on multimodal trajectory generation for autonomous delivery vehicle motion planning with Flow Matching, Energy Field modeling, and reinforcement learning. Contributed to Guide Flow Decoder design, FM pretraining on 120M trajectories, and FM + EF post-training on 7.5M samples. Proposed Hybrid KNN + epsilon-ball sampling to mitigate mode collapse, tuned dynamics parameters, optimized Anchor sampling and inference, and participated in closed-loop evaluation and deployment. The deployed model converges within 4 inference steps and supports intersection straight-driving, left-turn, and lane-change scenarios.
  • Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences 2025.11 – 2026.02
    Reinforcement Learning Algorithm Intern Advisor: Prof. Zhiqiang Pu · Beijing
    Optimized MARL algorithms for AirSim-based 3D pursuit-evasion tasks using a MAPPO pipeline for 3v1 and 5v2 settings with up to 80% success rate; built a DreamerV3 world-model framework for vision-only navigation with ArUco-guided target reaching and obstacle avoidance; and deployed embodied RL systems on RK3588 and ROS, including SAC-based autonomous obstacle avoidance on LingAo ground vehicles and a mocap-connected real-time perception-reasoning-control loop for Owl2Mini drones.
