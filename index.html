Publications

Spatial Sampling-based Passivity and Synchronization of Multiweighted Coupled Reaction-diffusion Neural Networks

【PDF】

Haodong Cui, Haoyun Tang, Mingyu Ma, Cheng Hu*, Tingting Shi

Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation (SCI Q2), 2025

This paper constructs a multi-weighted coupled reaction-diffusion neural network model integrating various time delays, establishes passivity and synchronization criteria applicable to systems with input-output dimension mismatch, and designs spatial point sampling controllers with adaptive control strategies. My contributions include theoretical derivation, image encryption applications, introduction writing, and numerical simulation sections.

Adaptive Passivity-based Synchronization of Spatiotemporal Neural Networks with Multi-weighted Coupling under Spatially Point Measurements

【PDF】

Haoyun Tang, Haodong Cui, Mingyu Ma, Cheng Hu*, Tingting Shi

Chinese Intelligent Systems Conference (CISC), 2025

This paper proposes adaptive sampling controllers that dynamically adjust control gains to achieve strict passivity and synchronization of multi-weighted coupled spatiotemporal networks. By employing vector rearrangement techniques, the strong connectivity requirement for each layer is relaxed to the irreducibility of the weighted union graph. My contributions include theoretical derivation, numerical simulation framework design, MATLAB code development, and paper writing.
