About Me

👋 Welcome to my personal homepage! I am Haoyun Tang, currently an M.S. student in Applied Mathematics at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU).

🔬 My research interests primarily focus on World Action Models, Reinforcement Learning, and Embodied Intelligence. I am dedicated to building world representations that let robots anticipate the consequences of their actions and refine them before execution.

🤖 Currently, I am an Embodied AI Research Intern at TuojingAI, mentored by Bowen Jing, and a World Model Research Intern at Nexus Lab, Peking University, advised by Bin Chong. Feel free to contact me!

📝 Publications

ThinkWAM: Rethinking Latent Futures for Action Refinement
Haoyun Tang*, Baqiao Deng*, Enjin Zhou, Songwen Tian, Keyao Xu, Bowen Jing, Kun Wang, Ke Ma, Zhandong Mei†, Huibin Li†
* Equal contribution. † Co-corresponding authors.
arXiv preprint
arXiv / Website / Code 4

A latent world-action model whose action proposal corrects multi-horizon future latents; the corrected futures then condition a second pass of the same action head to refine the executed chunk.

PhysFoundry: A Real2Sim2Real Dataset and Benchmark for Physics-Grounded Dexterous Manipulation
Bowen Jing*, Qi Song*, Haoyun Tang*, Haosong Sun, Zijian Zhang, Chenchen Ge, Mingxin Wang, Qiyang Zhou, Minglei Li, Weitao Zhou, Yuanxin Zhong, Haibao Yu†
* Equal contribution. † Corresponding author.
Under review

Grounds interactive assets and manipulation data in measured real-world physics across four capability dimensions, and evaluates both the training utility of generated data and the reliability of simulated evaluation against the real robot.

DriveAnchor: Progressive Anchor-based Flow Learning for Autonomous Driving Planning
Limin Yan*†, Haoyun Tang*, Yutao Qiu, Hongqing Liu, Zhandong Mei, Haoyu Xu‡
* Equal contribution. † Lead corresponding author. ‡ Co-corresponding author.
arXiv preprint
arXiv / alphaXiv 20 / Website

A three-stage planner built on a vocabulary of real trajectory anchors, combining residual Flow Matching, Energy-Field corridor guidance and anchor-guided zeroth-order reward fine-tuning.

CrashDojo: Accident-Grounded World Models as Closed-Loop Safety Examiners for Autonomous Driving Planners
Bowen Jing*, Haoyun Tang*, Ruiyang Hao, Chenchen Ge, Weitao Zhou, Haibao Yu†
* Equal contribution. † Corresponding author.
Under review

Turns accident evidence into explicit actor–mechanism requests and guides a frozen traffic world model to execute them in closed loop, adapting to the ego planner while keeping the intended maneuver and timing.

CounterScene: Counterfactual Causal Reasoning in Generative World Models for Safety-Critical Closed-Loop Traffic Scenario Generation
Bowen Jing*, Ruiyang Hao*, Haoyun Tang, Weitao Zhou, Haibao Yu†
* Equal contribution. † Corresponding author.
Under review

Uses the spatial and temporal safety margins of recorded encounters to guide generation: a progressive soft intervention shrinks these margins for one selected agent, while the world model generates the ego and surrounding traffic responses in closed loop.

HaM-World: Soft-Hamiltonian World Models with Selective Memory for Planning
Haoyun Tang*, Haodong Cui*, Keyao Xu, Zhandong Mei†, Kun Wang†
* Equal contribution. † Corresponding authors.
Accepted to NeurIPS 2026
arXiv / alphaXiv 18 / Code 15

Decomposes the latent state into a soft-Hamiltonian (q, p) space and a semantic context with selective memory, improving long-horizon rollout stability and OOD robustness for planning.
